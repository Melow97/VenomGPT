# Venom GPT Free vs Pro

## Free
- Core Venom GPT chat
- Basic workspace tools
- Basic productivity workflows
- Basic calendar/team-style productivity workflows
- Fictional Spider Tracker

## Pro
Pro is positioned as the power-user workspace for people who want Venom GPT to become a broader AI hub.

- Higher AI usage and capacity
- Advanced research workflows
- Larger file workflows
- Advanced voice-ready features
- Priority processing
- Connected-app integrations as enabled
- Developer and cloud workflows
- Collaboration and scheduling integrations
- More connectors added over time

## Connector positioning
Examples of future/available connector categories include GitHub, Cloudflare, Teams, Calendar and other productivity/developer services. Do not advertise an integration as fully live until its OAuth/API connection has been implemented and tested.

## Product messaging
Free should feel useful on its own. Pro should feel like the natural upgrade for users who want higher capacity, deeper workflows and connected apps in one Venom GPT workspace.
